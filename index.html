Frequently asked

How do I play?

Press anywhere on the screen and drag. Your shaman walks toward your finger and stops when you lift it. Attacking is fully automatic — you only decide where to stand.

What are runes and how does merging work?

Every time you level up you pick one of three runes and place it on the 3×3 tray. When two runes of the same kind and same level land on the same slot, they merge into one rune of the next level. Your build is not a list — it is a field you plant during the run.

What is the goal?

Survive until Sahmeran arrives at 2:30, then kill it. That wins the run.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes your gold and upgrades. There is no account and no cloud save, which also means we cannot restore progress for you. If progress disappeared without deleting the app, please write to us with your device model and iOS version.

Can I pause?

Yes. Tap the pause button at the top right of the run. You can resume or quit, and quitting keeps the gold you earned. The game also pauses itself when you leave the app.

The game is silent

KAM has almost no text on purpose, but it does have sound. Check the mute switch on your device, then Settings inside the game.

Accessibility

KAM respects Reduce Motion (screen shake turns off completely) and the HUD is labelled for VoiceOver. Every rune carries a shape and a I/II/III numeral as well as a colour, so colour is never the only signal. If something is unreadable for you, please tell us — we will fix it.

Nasıl oynanır?

Ekranın herhangi bir yerine bas ve sürükle. Kam parmağının gösterdiği yere yürür, parmağını kaldırınca durur. Saldırı tamamen otomatik — sen yalnız nerede duracağına karar veriyorsun.

Rün ve birleştirme nedir?

Her seviye atladığında üç rün arasından birini seçip 3×3 tepsiye yerleştiriyorsun. Aynı tür ve aynı seviyeden iki rün aynı slota gelirse birleşip bir üst seviyeye çıkıyor.

Amaç ne?

2:30'da gelen Şahmeran'a kadar hayatta kalmak, sonra onu öldürmek. Koşuyu kazandıran bu.
